MetaMask Practical – Step-by-Step Procedure
Aim: To create a MetaMask wallet, connect it to the Sepolia test network, perform ETH transactions, and calculate gas fees.
Step 1: Install MetaMask
1. Open the official website: https://metamask.io/
2. Click Download.
3. Select your browser (Google Chrome).
4. Click Add to Chrome.
5. Click Add Extension.
6. Pin the MetaMask extension to your browser toolbar.

Step 2: Create a New Wallet
1. Open the MetaMask extension.
2. Click Create a new wallet.
3. Agree to the terms and conditions.
4. Set a strong password.
5. MetaMask will generate a 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase.
6. Write the phrase on paper and confirm it when asked.
Important: Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase with anyone. Never take a screenshot or store it online.

Step 3: Enable Sepolia Test Network
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Select Advanced.
4. Enable Show test networks.
5. Return to the wallet.
6. Open the network dropdown.
7. Select Sepolia.
Sepolia is an Ethereum test network used for practicing blockchain transactions without spending real ETH.
Step 4: Get Free Test ETH
1. Copy your MetaMask account address (starting with 0x).
2. Open a Sepolia faucet. (https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia)
3. Paste your wallet address.
4. Complete any required verification.
5. Request free Sepolia test ETH.
6. Wait for the transaction to complete.
7. Check your MetaMask wallet balance.
Faucet websites:
- Google Cloud Sepolia Faucet
- Alchemy Sepolia Faucet
Step 5: Send ETH to Another Account
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Click Send.
3. Enter the recipient's wallet address.
4. Enter the amount: 0.001 ETH.
5. Click Next.
6. Review the transaction details.
7. Confirm the transaction.
Step 6: Check Gas Fee Details
Before confirming the transaction:
1. Open the gas fee or Edit option.
2. Observe the following parameters:
   - Gas Fee: Estimated transaction cost.
   - Gas Limit: Maximum amount of gas allowed.
   - Max Base Fee: Maximum base fee per gas you are willing to pay.
   - Priority Fee: Additional tip paid to the validator.
Step 7: Repeat Transactions Using Three Speed Options
Perform the transaction using these three options:
1. Low
2. Market
3. Aggressive
Record the estimated gas fee for each option.

Step 8: View Transaction Details
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Navigate to Activity.
3. Select your completed transaction.
4. Click View on block explorer.
5. The Sepolia Etherscan page will open.
6. Record the following details:

Step 9: Calculate and Compare Transaction Fees
Formula:

Transaction Fee = Gas Used * Gas Price


Example:
- Gas Used = 21,000
- Gas Price = 10 Gwei
